Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking farm machinery, hiring laborers, and payment safety on KhetSaathi.

When a farmer selects machinery and specifies a date range, KhetSaathi runs an atomic database transaction with concurrency locking to check for overlapping active rentals. Once payment is confirmed, those specific dates are locked exclusively for your booking, ensuring no other farmer can reserve the same equipment on those days.

To ensure fair availability for subsequent farmers, equipment returned after the scheduled end date incurs a standardized late fee:
Late Fee = (Days Past Return Date) × (Daily Rental Rate × 1.5)
If returned on or before the due date, no late fee is applied.

Yes! In your "My Bookings" screen, click Extend Booking. You can extend by 1 to 30 days. The system immediately checks that no other farmer has reserved the machinery during the requested extension days and recalculates your total based on the standard daily rate.

Farmers and owners can cancel any booking in Pending or Confirmed status prior to the physical equipment handover. Once the status transitions to Ongoing, the rental cannot be cancelled mid-operation.

KhetSaathi uses Razorpay payment gateway integration with HMAC-SHA256 signature verification. All card details, UPI transactions, and net banking operations are handled directly through RBI-compliant secure channels. KhetSaathi does not store raw credit card or banking credentials.

Owners receive instant in-app alerts on their notification bell and dashboard. Once payment is confirmed, the owner inspects the request and clicks Handover (Ongoing) when handing over the machinery to the farmer.
